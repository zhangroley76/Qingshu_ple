Qingshu (青书) is a free, open-source input method for Android phones with a physical keyboard, made on the Unihertz Titan 2 Elite. Everything is typed and chosen from the keys, without touching the screen, and all processing is offline: no account, no analytics, no network access needed for typing.

<b>Chinese (pinyin)</b>
<ul>
<li>Whole-sentence pinyin from the open-source Qingjian engine</li>
<li>Pick candidates 1–9 with Alt + the keys that carry the digits; Space takes the highlighted one</li>
<li>Offline next-word association, learning of the words you pick</li>
<li>Traditional output (Taiwan / Hong Kong forms), fuzzy pinyin, initials-only input</li>
<li>Domain dictionaries (idioms, places, IT, medicine, law…), custom phrases (type a code, get your e-mail address)</li>
</ul>

<b>Cantonese, Korean, Japanese (optional)</b>
<ul>
<li>Cantonese: Jyutping without tones (neihou → 你好), initials work too; lexicon from rime-cantonese</li>
<li>Korean: 2-set layout</li>
<li>Japanese: romaji → kana → kanji, word by word, from the SKK dictionary</li>
</ul>

<b>English</b>
<ul>
<li>Autocorrect tuned to the keyboard's layout (from Numen); Backspace right after a fix undoes it</li>
</ul>

<b>Keyboard</b>
<ul>
<li>All of Plektra's hardware-keyboard features: modifiers, SYM pages, navigation mode, snippets, clipboard history</li>
<li>Shift/Alt swap, Fn as Ctrl with arrow keys on Fn + E S D F</li>
<li>Type /? in any text field for the list of shortcuts</li>
</ul>

The APK is large (about 95 MB) because the Chinese dictionaries, the language model and the Cantonese and Japanese dictionaries are bundled for offline use.

Qingshu is a fork of Plektra (the successor of Pastiera). It is not an official release of Plektra, Qingjian or Numen.
